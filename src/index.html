Methodology

The Inflation Explorer uses World Bank data (annual average consumer prices, 2010–2024). It compounds each year's rate to estimate how the price level changed between two years. The official table above uses December-to-December changes, so the two series differ slightly (for example, 2022 is 15.0% average-year but 20.3% Dec to Dec). Both are correct; they measure different things. Compounding annual rates is an approximation of a full price index.

Sources: Bureau of National Statistics · World Bank via FRED · National Bank of Kazakhstan
